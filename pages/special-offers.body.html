Offer terms

One coupon per household per visit. Coupons cannot be combined with each other, but they can be combined with financing and with maintenance-plan member discounts where noted. Discounts are applied to the labour and service portion of the invoice; sales tax and permit fees are calculated after the discount.

Because our technicians quote a flat price before starting. If a discount is added afterwards, the office has to reissue paperwork and the technician's parts order may already be in motion. Telling us when you book keeps the whole visit clean.

Seasonal offers run from the first of the month shown through the last day of that season. The three headline coupons — SAVE50, SAVE100 and SAVEALL — run continuously, and we update this page rather than letting an old offer linger. If it is on this page, it is live.

Utility and manufacturer rebates change with the season and depend on the efficiency rating you choose. We submit the paperwork on your behalf and pass the money straight through — see the financing page for the current list of eligible programs.
