Why this unit for a Joliet house

A 2.5 ton (30,000 BTU/hr) condenser is the workhorse size for the ranch and two-story homes built across Will County from the 1960s through the 2000s. It pairs with a 3-ton nominal coil and a 60k–80k BTU furnace — the combination our installers see in the majority of local basements.

Single-stage cooling means the compressor runs at one speed: full power, then off. It is the simplest, longest-lived design on the market, it is inexpensive to repair, and in our climate it dehumidifies well because it runs longer cycles on our humid summer days.
