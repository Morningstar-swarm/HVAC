Our story

Started with one van and a lot of furnace calls

We opened on the west side of Joliet doing repairs and nothing else. No new construction contracts, no builder work — just service calls, mostly in the winter, mostly on systems somebody else had installed badly.

That turned out to be the best possible training. By the time we started replacing equipment, we knew exactly which parts fail, which installations create callbacks, and how much of what customers were being sold was unnecessary. Those lessons shaped the way we quote today: flat prices, written findings, and the cheapest repair that will actually hold.

Fifteen years later we run multiple service vans out of 1504 Essington Rd, cover six counties, and still answer the after-hours line the same way we did in the first year — with a person, not a queue.

What we stand on

Four rules we don't bend

Price before work

Flat-rate pricing quoted and approved before a single tool comes out. No hourly meters, no "while we're in there" surprises.

Plain language

You'll get meter readings and photos, not jargon. If we can't explain why a part needs replacing, we shouldn't be replacing it.

Repair first

We quote the repair even when a replacement pays us more. Plenty of 20-year-old furnaces have good years left in them.

Answer the phone

24 hours, every day, a human being in Joliet. It is the lowest-tech promise we make and the one customers mention most.
