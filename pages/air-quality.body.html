A 1" MERV-13 filter in a slot designed for a thin fibreglass filter is the single most common air-quality mistake we see — it starves the blower and freezes coils. The right answer is usually a 4" or 5" media cabinet with more surface area and less pressure drop.

An in-duct lamp aimed at the evaporator coil keeps the wettest, darkest part of your system from growing biological slime — which is what generates that musty smell when the A/C starts in June.

  • Installed: $480–$1,150 depending on lamp count
  • Lamp replaced every 12–18 months
  • Runs on about 30 watts
  • Safe to run continuously with the blower

Illinois winters routinely drop indoor humidity into the low 20s. A bypass or steam humidifier brings it back to 35–45%, which is the range where you stop getting shocked by doorknobs and your wood floors stop separating.

  • Bypass humidifier installed: $520–$850
  • Steam humidifier installed: $1,450–$2,400
  • Whole-home dehumidifier: $1,300–$2,900
  • Humidity setpoint controlled from the thermostat

Leaky joints in an unconditioned attic or crawlspace waste a measurable slice of every heating dollar, and crushed flex runs in a finished basement can leave three rooms fighting for air.

  • Mastic and mesh sealing of accessible joints
  • Flex duct replacement in attics and crawlspaces
  • Adding returns where the system is starving
  • Balancing dampers so the furthest room gets its share

Typical range: $650–$4,200 depending on access and scope.
