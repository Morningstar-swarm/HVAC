Repairs & diagnosis

When something's broken

Our rule of thumb: if the system is 12+ years old, needs a compressor, coil or heat exchanger, and the repair costs more than a third of a new system, replacement usually wins. Everything else, repair. We put both numbers side by side and let you choose.

Turn it off at the thermostat — and for the day, switch the fan to ON so the coil thaws. Running a frozen system can slug the compressor with liquid refrigerant, which turns a $200 airflow problem into a $2,000 compressor replacement. Thawing takes 2–4 hours.

A faint dust smell during the first heat of the season is normal and burns off in a few minutes. A sharp electrical smell, or smoke, means shut the system off at the breaker and call us — that's a blower motor, a control board or a wiring fault, and it doesn't improve with use.

Maintenance

Servicing, filters and plans

Once a year each: cooling in spring, heating in early fall. It keeps manufacturer warranties valid, catches combustion problems before they become safety issues, and is the cheapest way to add years to equipment life.

MERV 8 is plenty for a standard 1" slot and won't starve the blower. If you want MERV 11 or 13 for allergies, move to a 4" or 5" media cabinet where the surface area is bigger. Never squeeze a thick filter into a thin slot.
