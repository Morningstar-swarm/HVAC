Commercial services

What we install, repair and maintain

Open a line item for scope, typical cost and how quickly we can get parts.

Gas/electric and heat-pump rooftops, curb adapters, economisers and crane-scheduled change-outs. We stage the crane, coordinate the roof permit and have the old unit recycled the same day.

  • Repair range: $480–$3,600 depending on part
  • Replacement: $9,500–$24,000 installed
  • Compressors, boards, economiser actuators in stock locally
  • Belt, filter and coil service on a scheduled basis

Suite-by-suite comfort where a landlord pays for common areas but tenants control their own space. Mini-split and VRF options avoid ductwork entirely in retrofit buildings.

  • Installed: $4,800–$18,000 per suite
  • Individual metering and controls
  • Quiet enough for medical and dental offices

If your kitchen hood sucks the front door shut, the make-up air is wrong. We balance exhaust and supply so the dining room stays comfortable and the hood still passes inspection.

  • Belt and direct-drive fans
  • Gas and electric make-up air units
  • Test-and-balance with written readings
  • Grease-duct cleaning coordination

Cast-iron sectional, condensing and steam boilers, plus pumps, expansion tanks, air separators and driveways that need to stay clear without a plough contract.

  • Annual boiler inspection and combustion setup
  • Pump and zone valve replacement
  • Glycol snow-melt commissioning and controls

Standalone thermostats, programmable schedules and cloud-connected controllers so you can see a rooftop failure before your tenants call you about it.

  • Alarm and trend notifications by text or email
  • Lockout limits for after-hours setpoints
  • No monthly fee on our standard controllers

Condensing units, evaporators, defrost controls and door heaters for walk-ins and reach-ins — handled through our refrigeration partners when the job needs EPA-certified refrigeration work beyond our scope.

  • Temperature monitoring and alarm relays
  • Coil cleaning and drain service
  • Emergency calls with product-loss priority
