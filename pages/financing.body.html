How it works

Four steps, and only one of them is paperwork

  • Tell us about the job

    Call 815-556-0660 or book online. We quote the work first — financing is decided after you know the price.

  • Pre-qualify in minutes

Rebates & incentives

Money that's already set aside for you

Utilities and manufacturers both pay for efficiency upgrades in Illinois. It is genuinely free money — the catch is the paperwork, which we do for you.

Illinois efficiency programs typically pay $300–$1,100 on qualifying high-efficiency furnaces, A/C units, heat pumps and smart thermostats. Amounts depend on efficiency rating and household income tier, and they change each program year.

  • We confirm eligibility before you sign anything
  • Installer and model numbers documented on the invoice
  • Submission handled by our office
  • Rebate applied to your balance or paid to you directly

Equipment brands run seasonal promotions — commonly $200–$1,000 when a qualifying indoor and outdoor unit are installed together, and sometimes a free thermostat or extended warranty. We tell you which promotion is live on the day you book, and we never invent one.

Energy-efficiency improvements may qualify under federal tax provisions and can affect the basis of your home when you sell it. We provide the itemised invoice with model and efficiency ratings that your accountant will ask for, and we're happy to email a copy years later.

Storm, lightning, hail and water damage claims are common in northeast Illinois. We document the failure with photos and meter readings and send the report directly to your adjuster, which tends to move claims along faster.
