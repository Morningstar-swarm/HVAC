Sizing, not guessing

Why "the same as the old one" is often wrong

Equipment gets replaced over and over at whatever size the first contractor picked in 1987 — before the windows were upgraded, before the attic was insulated, before the addition went on. An oversized A/C cools too fast and leaves the house clammy; an oversized furnace short-cycles and cracks heat exchangers.

  • 📐Room-by-room calculation using window area, orientation, insulation values and ceiling height.
  • 🌬️Static pressure and airflow testing to prove the existing ducts can move the air the new system needs.
  • 🔍Combustion and venting review so the new furnace vents correctly — a common hidden cost on old chimney systems.
  • 📄Written findings. You keep the calculation sheet whether you hire us or not.
